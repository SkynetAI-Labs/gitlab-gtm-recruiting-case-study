## The challenge
Build commercially credible teams across Account Executives, Solutions Architects, Customer Success, Sales Development and Sales Operations, while keeping distributed hiring teams aligned and candidates informed.

## My contribution
I bring 20+ years of full-cycle recruitment, international hiring and business development. Recruiting sales managers, sales engineers and business development professionals for industrial and energy clients gives me a practical starting point for technical-commercial hiring. I would combine that experience with learning GitLab’s customers, sales segments and operating practices.

## How I would run the work
1. Agree territory, outcomes, essential competencies, interview responsibilities and timelines at intake.
2. Map direct and adjacent talent through balanced sourcing channels.
3. Test personal contribution with structured questions and specialist interview partners.
4. Keep records, next actions, owners and candidate communication dates clear.
5. Explain offer assumptions and unresolved questions before asking for a decision.

## The judgment example
A seller at 126% attainment in an established territory and a seller at 91% in a new territory both deserve contextual investigation. I would validate quota definitions, ramp, inherited accounts, personal deal ownership and repeatability against the agreed brief. The headline percentage alone is insufficient to recommend one over the other.

## First 90 days
- **Days 1–30:** learn the business and calibrate briefs, scorecards, record keeping and communication routines.
- **Days 31–60:** run balanced searches, structured screens and concise written risk updates.
- **Days 61–90:** review source quality, stage delays, offer questions and candidate feedback; document an improvement others can reuse.

**Next step:** pressure-test the AE comparison and one live hiring brief with the GTM recruiting team in week one.
