# Five role-specific interview kits

Proposed hiring resources, not GitLab internal interview plans.

## Account Executive

**Outcome:** Build pipeline and lead complex enterprise opportunities.

**Intake:** Agree territory maturity, named accounts, quota definition, ramp treatment, sales cycle and specialist support.

### Competencies

- Commercial discovery

- Deal ownership

- Forecast discipline

- Technical partnership

### Questions

- Walk me through a complex deal you personally won. Who controlled budget, what changed during procurement and what was your contribution?

- Compare your last two quota years. How did territory, ramp and inherited accounts affect the result?

- Tell me about a deal you removed from forecast. What evidence changed your view?

**Work example:** Map the buying group for a fictional customer with fragmented development and security tools.

## Solutions Architect

**Outcome:** Translate discovery into a credible customer evaluation.

**Intake:** Agree discovery ownership, technical depth, evaluation success criteria, travel and interviewer responsibilities.

### Competencies

- Technical discovery

- Evaluation design

- Security awareness

- Commercial partnership

### Questions

- Describe an evaluation where the initial requirement was not the real customer problem. What changed?

- How did you agree success criteria, owners and a decision date for a proof of value?

- When did you advise a seller that the proposed solution was not ready for the customer?

**Work example:** Scope an evaluation with a technical interviewer, explaining assumptions and acceptance criteria.

## Customer Success

**Outcome:** Connect adoption to value and surface renewal risk early.

**Intake:** Clarify adoption versus renewal ownership, account load, customer maturity and partnership with account executives.

### Competencies

- Success planning

- Risk diagnosis

- Stakeholder engagement

- Value communication

### Questions

- Walk me through an at-risk account. Which signals appeared first and what action did you own?

- How did you connect adoption to an outcome the customer valued?

- Describe a difficult renewal. What belonged to you and what belonged to the account executive?

**Work example:** Write a recovery plan for low adoption before renewal, separating facts from hypotheses.

## Sales Development

**Outcome:** Create relevant, qualified conversations and accurate handoffs.

**Intake:** Agree inbound/outbound split, accepted-opportunity definition, target accounts, coaching and ramp expectations.

### Competencies

- Account research

- Relevant outreach

- Qualification

- Learning agility

### Questions

- How would you research an account and turn that into a short relevant message?

- Tell me about a meeting an AE rejected. What did you learn about qualification?

- Which change improved accepted opportunities, and how did you distinguish quality from activity?

**Work example:** Prepare one message and a handoff note from a fictional account brief; offer an accessible format.

## Sales Operations

**Outcome:** Improve reporting trust and remove a measurable process friction.

**Intake:** Agree systems scope, data access, territory versus compensation responsibilities and change ownership.

### Competencies

- Data quality

- Process diagnosis

- Stakeholder influence

- Change control

### Questions

- Describe a reporting discrepancy. How did you trace its cause and restore trust?

- Tell me about a territory or process change sales leaders resisted. How did you influence it?

- How would you validate a dashboard before leaders used it for decisions?

**Work example:** Investigate a fictional CRM extract and recommend one controlled improvement.

## Debrief record

Record the specific example, contribution, context and outcome. Distinguish evidence from inference. Use Not yet evidenced / Partial / Clear / Strong as discussion anchors. Note missing evidence, an owner and a proportionate follow-up. Keep decisions with the hiring team.
